## Heritage Services

Heritage Services runs **[Agent Index](https://theagentindex.io)**, a directory of AI agents and audit firms for compliance, security and GRC teams. Each listing cites its sources and shows a last-checked date. Listings are not ranked or scored.

- [AI agents](https://theagentindex.io/agents/): compliance, security, risk and privacy agents, mapped to frameworks such as SOC 2, ISO 27001, HIPAA and DPDP
- [Audit firms](https://theagentindex.io/auditors/): firms checked against official registries (ANAB, UKAS, PCI SSC, HITRUST, FedRAMP, Cyber AB, CERT-In)
- [Alternatives to Vanta](https://theagentindex.io/alternatives/vanta/) · [Alternatives to Drata](https://theagentindex.io/alternatives/drata/)
- [Guides](https://theagentindex.io/guides/)
- [Open dataset](https://theagentindex.io/data/) (CC BY 4.0)
- [Listing badge](https://theagentindex.io/badge/) for vendors and audit firms listed on Agent Index
